AXON 1Pro Solutions · Philexcel HUB

Set up or repair your office network drives

Connect your Windows computer to the Philexcel office network. Choose the automatic assistant or follow the manual mapping guide. Use your own Philexcel NAS username and password, not your Microsoft 365 credentials.

Office NAS connectivity check

The AXON Windows Setup & Repair assistant automatically checks whether PHILEXCEL-HUB is reachable from your computer. If your NAS is available, it continues with your personal NAS login and drive setup. If the NAS is unavailable, it explains the issue and stops safely.

01 · Automatic setup & repair

Recommended for all employees. Use the same assistant on your first day or whenever a network drive goes missing.

  1. Connect to the office Wi-Fi or wired office network.
  2. Download the AXON batch installer below.
  3. Double-click the file and read the welcome and safety information.
  4. Enter your personal Philexcel NAS username and password when requested. Never send passwords to support.
  5. Review the drive audit and approve only the repairs you want.
  6. Open This PC and check your connected drives after the final report.

Download Windows Setup & Repair (.bat)

The installer checks the NAS on your computer. If PHILEXCEL-HUB cannot be reached, it stops and explains how to get help. It does not require Microsoft 365 sign-in.

02 · Manual setup (advanced)

If you prefer not to run an installer, map your approved shares manually in Windows File Explorer.

  1. Open File Explorer → This PC.
  2. Choose More (…) → Map network drive (or Computer → Map network drive).
  3. Select the drive letter listed below and enter the exact network path.
  4. Select Reconnect at sign-in. If Windows asks, use your personal NAS account to authenticate.
  5. Repeat only for other folders your NAS account is authorized to access.
DriveOffice path
X:\\PHILEXCEL-HUB\Executive
H:\\PHILEXCEL-HUB\AdminHR

These are standard company drive letters, not a grant of access. Your NAS permissions determine which folders can be opened. Do not map another employee's restricted share. If a drive letter is already occupied, contact support before replacing it.

Not connected to the office network?

Sorry, the Philexcel NAS assistant can only map drives when your PC can reach the PHILEXCEL-HUB server. Join the approved office network and try again, or contact your company administrator / AXON support. The website itself cannot securely detect your private LAN or authenticate your NAS account.

If you need cloud files while away from the office, use data.philexcel.com and your approved Microsoft 365 access.
