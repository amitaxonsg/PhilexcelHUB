Set up company folders without Microsoft 365

Back to enrollment

You need a personal NAS username, password and an administrator-confirmed department. This public guide cannot verify your identity, look up your department or promise access. Your NAS permissions decide which folders you can open.

Check before changing anything

Download Windows drive check or Download Mac connection check. Both are read-only and show results on your device. They do not upload your drive list.

Do not disconnect every network drive. Keep matching working mappings. Close files before replacing a conflicting drive. Ask support if you are unsure.

Windows without a helper

  1. Connect to the office network.
  2. Open File Explorer → This PC and review existing network drives.
  3. If your intended letter points elsewhere, note its old path. Close files, then disconnect only that mapping if approved.
  4. Choose Map network drive from the toolbar or three-dot menu.
  5. Select your department letter from the guide and enter its network path.
  6. Choose Connect using different credentials if needed. Enter your personal NAS username and password.
  7. Choose Reconnect at sign-in if wanted and finish.
  8. Repeat for CompanyShared using Z:. If a letter is a local disk, stop and ask support.

Away from the office, use approved cloud or remote access; do not expose SMB to the internet.

Mac without a helper

  1. Connect to the office network.
  2. Check Finder Locations for existing company volumes.
  3. Keep a matching volume. To replace an old connection, close files and eject only that old volume.
  4. Open Finder → Go → Connect to Server, or press Command K.
  5. Enter smb://{{ nas }}.
  6. Choose Registered User and enter your personal NAS username and password.
  7. Select your permitted department folder and CompanyShared.
  8. Drag mounted folders into the Finder sidebar. Mac does not use Windows drive letters.

Optional helper without Microsoft 365

Enter the details your administrator provided. These are your own entries, not verified eligibility. This form does not create users, change permissions or save a staff registration. The helper still requires successful NAS access.

Windows: review the file; use Run with PowerShell only when support approves the signed helper. Mac: open Terminal, type bash and a space, drag the file in and press Return. Do not bypass device security if blocked. Use the manual steps above instead. These helpers are for commissioning until device testing is complete.
