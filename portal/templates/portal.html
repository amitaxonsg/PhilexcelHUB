Mac

  1. Connect to the office network.

Add a staff member

  1. Confirm their full name, Microsoft 365 sign-in, department and manager’s approval.
  2. Create their personal NAS account through DSM. Give them a unique password.
  3. Add them to their department group and Company-Shared. Department members receive read/write access. Do not add them to administrators.
  4. Add their Microsoft 365 account to the matching HUB department group and Company-Shared in SharePoint.
  5. Ask Axon to add their verified Microsoft identity and NAS username to the enrollment register.
  6. Send them enroll.philexcel.com and test that another department is denied.

Move department or remove access

Remove old NAS and SharePoint memberships before adding the new department. For leavers, disable the NAS account, revoke Microsoft 365 sessions as appropriate, disable the enrollment record and remove group access. Ask Axon to review the change.

Recover a deleted file

Use the affected NAS share’s recycle bin or SharePoint’s recycle bin according to where the deletion occurred. Administrators can review all authorized departmental bins. Test recovery before deleting business data.
